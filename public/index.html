01 / The mission

Make every
step count.

Where would you go if your next step was on Mars? Ares Path is a student exploration concept that brings the question down to the surface: choose a destination, understand the terrain, and make time for the journey home.

Our starting point is Jezero Crater. Explore the mission sources behind the idea, then try a simple Marswalk time-budget demonstration.
